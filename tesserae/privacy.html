Tesserae 隱私政策

生效日：2026-10-07（購買與訂閱段落於同日加入，並更新為月訂閱、年訂閱與買斷）

Tesserae（以下簡稱「本 App」）是一個個人學習工具，用來管理課表、筆記、作業與複習。以下用白話說明它怎麼對待你的資料。

1. 我們蒐集什麼？

什麼都不蒐集。你的課表、筆記、錄音、照片、手寫與待辦只存在你自己的裝置上。我們沒有任何會接收你資料的伺服器，沒有帳號系統，沒有廣告，也沒有分析或追蹤工具。

2. 你可以自己選擇開啟的功能

  • 雲端備份與同步（選用）：如果你自己連上 Google 帳號，本 App 會把資料加密後存進你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟（僅限本 App 專屬的隱藏資料夾）。用來備份，或在你的另一台裝置接力使用。加密用的金鑰存放在你裝置的系統鑰匙圈，我們無法讀取。Google 登入與儲存同時受 Google 的隱私政策規範。不連的話，這項功能完全不會運作。
  • 練字字形資料：使用「練字」功能時，App 會向公開的內容傳遞網路 jsDelivr 下載筆順字形檔。這個請求不含你的任何資料，但對方可能看到你的 IP 位址。
  • AI 輔助（選用，預設關閉）：本 App 不提供任何 AI 金鑰。只有在你自己輸入某一家 AI 服務的金鑰（例如 Claude、ChatGPT、Gemini，或你自己填的相容服務），並在第一次使用前按下「同意並繼續」之後才會啟用。啟用後，只有你當次送出的問題、筆記文字、圖片或錄音轉出的文字，會直接從你的裝置傳給你選擇的 AI 供應商，依該供應商的條款處理。這些內容不會經過我們，我們不保存。你的金鑰只存在你的裝置上，不會寫進備份或雲端。你可以隨時在「設定 → AI 家教」撤回同意或關掉 AI，關掉後不會再送出任何內容。
  • 語音轉文字、手寫與文字辨識：優先使用你裝置系統內建的功能。若你的裝置不支援在裝置上辨識某種語言，系統可能改用 Apple 的語音辨識服務，這時依 Apple 的政策處理。

3. 權限

  • 麥克風：課堂錄音與口說練習。錄音存在你的裝置上；錄音中畫面上方會顯示「錄音中」。
  • 語音辨識：把錄音或你說的話轉成文字。
  • 相機與照片：拍下課本、板書或手寫筆記放進筆記，並把照片上的字轉成文字；也可把筆記圖片存到「照片」。
  • 通知：課程與待辦提醒。

這些權限都只在你用到那個功能時才會請求，你可以隨時到手機的「設定」收回。

購買與訂閱

App 可以免費下載。要繼續新增與編輯，可以選「月訂閱」「年訂閱」（自動續訂），或「買斷」（一次付清，含 Tesserae 與語言進化）；訂閱可能有免費試用，試用結束前沒有取消就會自動開始收費。購買由 Apple 處理，我們看不到你的付款資料。App 只在你的裝置上記錄「最後確認的時間」與目前的訂閱或買斷狀態（是否有效、到期時間），用來決定哪些功能可以使用、避免改時鐘；這些紀錄不會傳給我們。訂閱結束後 App 變成唯讀：你的資料仍在，可以查看、搜尋、匯出、備份與刪除。要管理或取消訂閱，請到 iPhone／iPad 的「設定」→ 你的名字 →「訂閱項目」，或在 App 內按「管理訂閱」。

4. 保存與刪除

資料保存在你的裝置上，直到你刪除。你可以在 App 內的「設定 → 資料與備份 → 刪除我的資料」：

  • 刪除這台裝置上的全部資料；
  • 刪除存在你 Google 帳號裡、由本 App 建立的同步與備份資料，並解除綁定。

解除安裝 App 也會移除裝置上的資料。你曾使用的 Google 或 AI 供應商那邊的資料，請到各服務自己管理或刪除。

5. 兒童

本 App 不會刻意向 13 歲以下的兒童蒐集資料。由於我們本來就不蒐集任何資料，也沒有帳號，未成年人使用時資料同樣只留在自己的裝置上。

6. 政策變更

如果這份政策有更動，會更新上面的生效日，並在 App 裡說明。

Privacy Policy for Tesserae

Effective date: 2026-10-07

The app (the "App") is a personal study tool for timetables, notes, assignments and review. This page explains in plain language how it handles your data.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Your timetable, notes, recordings, photos, handwriting and to-dos stay on your own device. We run no server that receives your data, have no account system, show no ads, and use no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Optional features you choose to turn on

  • Cloud backup and sync (optional). If you connect your own Google account, the App stores your data, encrypted, in your own Google Drive (in the App's private app-data folder only) for backup or for continuing on another of your devices. The encryption key is kept in your device's system Keychain, and we cannot read it. Google sign-in and storage are also governed by Google's privacy policy. If you do not connect an account, this feature does nothing.
  • Penmanship stroke data. When you use Penmanship, the App downloads stroke-order files from the public content network jsDelivr. The request contains none of your data, but that service may see your IP address.
  • AI assistance (optional, off by default). The App provides no AI key of its own. It works only after you enter your own key for an AI service (for example Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or a compatible service you enter yourself) and tap "Agree and continue" before first use. Then only what you send in that request (a question, note text, an image, or text transcribed from a recording) goes directly from your device to the AI provider you chose, and is handled under that provider's terms. It does not pass through us and we do not store it. Your key stays on your device only and is not written to backups or the cloud. You can withdraw consent or turn AI off at any time in Settings > AI Tutor; nothing is sent while it is off.
  • Speech-to-text and handwriting/text recognition. The App prefers features built into your device's operating system. If your device cannot recognize a language on-device, the system may use Apple's speech recognition service, in which case Apple's policies apply.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: class recording and speaking practice. Recordings stay on your device; a "Recording" indicator is shown at the top of the screen while recording.
  • Speech recognition: turning recordings or your speech into text.
  • Camera and Photos: capturing textbooks, whiteboards or handwritten notes into your notes and recognizing the text; saving note images to Photos.
  • Notifications: class and to-do reminders.

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature, and you can revoke it in system Settings at any time.

Purchases and subscriptions

The app is free to download. To keep adding and editing you can choose a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase (covers Tesserae and its language app). A subscription may include a free trial; if you do not cancel before the trial ends it starts charging automatically. Purchases are handled by Apple; we cannot see your payment details. The app only records on your device the last time it checked and your current subscription or purchase status (whether it is active and when it ends), to decide which features are available and to prevent clock tampering; these records are not sent to us. When a subscription ends the app becomes read-only: your data stays, and you can still view, search, export, back up and delete it. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad > your name > Subscriptions, or tap Manage Subscription in the app.

4. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. In the App, go to Settings > Data & Backup > "Delete my data" to:

  • delete all data on this device; or
  • delete the sync and backup data this App created in your Google account, and unlink it.

Uninstalling the App also removes the data on your device. For data held by Google or AI providers you used, manage or delete it on those services.

5. Children

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Because we collect no data and have no accounts, a minor's data likewise stays only on their own device.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the change described in the App.
