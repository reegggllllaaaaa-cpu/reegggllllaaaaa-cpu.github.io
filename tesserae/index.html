Tesserae 支援

手寫・錄音・學習筆記。這裡整理常見問題。找不到答案，請看最下面的聯絡方式。

第一次使用，想先看看

在 App 裡到「設定 → 關於 → 載入範例資料」，會放進一組虛構的課表、待辦與筆記，每個主要畫面都看得到。同一處可以一鍵清空範例，不會動到你自己的資料。

我的資料存在哪裡？

預設只存在你的裝置上，不需要註冊帳號。詳情見隱私權政策。換新裝置或想備份，可以在 App 內設定備份（目前已確認的選項是連上你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟，加密後存放）。刪除資料：「設定 → 資料管理 → 刪除我的資料」。

購買、試用與訂閱

  • App 可以免費下載。要繼續新增與編輯，可選月訂閱、年訂閱（皆自動續訂），或一次付清的買斷。訂閱可能有 1 週免費試用。
  • 試用或訂閱結束後，你的資料仍在，可以查看、搜尋、匯出與備份，只是不能新增或編輯；購買後馬上恢復。
  • 恢復購買：在 App 內購買畫面按「恢復購買」，或到「設定 → 關於 → 購買與解鎖」。
  • 取消訂閱：在 iPhone／iPad 開啟「設定」→ 你的名字 →「訂閱項目」，選 Tesserae 後取消；或在 App 內按「管理訂閱」。請在下次續訂前至少 24 小時取消。
  • 退款：購買由 Apple 處理，請至 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。

權限為什麼要開？

  • 麥克風：課堂錄音與口說練習。
  • 語音辨識：把錄音轉成逐字稿（裝置支援時在裝置上辨識，不支援時系統可能改用 Apple 的語音辨識服務）。
  • 相機：拍講義、板書放進筆記並辨識文字。
  • 照片（僅新增）：把筆記圖片存到「照片」。
  • 通知：課程與待辦提醒。

每項都只在你用到那個功能時才詢問，可以隨時到系統「設定」收回。

AI 功能

AI 家教為選用、預設關閉。App 不附任何 AI 金鑰；要自己輸入某個 AI 服務的金鑰，並在第一次使用前同意，之後只有你按下去的那一次才會把內容直接送到你選的供應商。不用 AI，所有核心功能照常使用。

常見狀況

  • 錄音沒有逐字稿：逐字稿需要語音辨識權限；請確認系統「設定 → Tesserae」裡麥克風與語音辨識都開著。
  • 手寫延遲或不跟手：請確認使用 Apple Pencil 並更新到最新的 iPadOS。仍有問題請寄信並附上機型與系統版本。
  • 換裝置：先在舊裝置完成備份，再到新裝置還原。

Tesserae Support

Study Notes: Write & Record. Common questions are below. If you cannot find your answer, see Contact at the bottom.

Just want to look around first

In the app, go to Settings > About > Load Sample Data. It fills the app with a made-up timetable, to-dos and notes so every main screen has something to show. The same place clears the samples in one tap and never touches your own data.

Where is my data?

By default only on your device, and no account is needed. See the Privacy Policy. To back up or move to a new device you can set up a backup in the app (the confirmed option today is connecting your own Google Drive, where it is stored encrypted). To delete everything: Settings > Data > Delete My Data.

Purchases, trial and subscriptions

  • The app is free to download. To keep adding and editing you can choose a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions may include a 1-week free trial.
  • When a trial or subscription ends, your data stays: you can still view, search, export and back up. Only adding and editing pause, and they resume immediately after you purchase.
  • Restore purchases: tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen, or go to Settings > About > Purchases and unlock.
  • Cancel a subscription: on your iPhone or iPad open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose Tesserae and cancel; or tap Manage Subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why does the app ask for permissions?

  • Microphone: class recording and speaking practice.
  • Speech recognition: turning recordings into transcripts (on-device when your device supports the language; otherwise the system may use Apple's speech recognition service).
  • Camera: capture handouts and whiteboards into notes and recognize the text.
  • Photos (add only): save note images to Photos.
  • Notifications: class and to-do reminders.

Each is requested only when you use the related feature, and you can revoke it in system Settings at any time.

AI features

The AI tutor is optional and off by default. The app includes no AI key; you enter your own key for an AI service and agree before first use, and after that content is sent directly to the provider you chose, only when you tap. Every core feature works without AI.

Troubleshooting

  • No transcript for a recording: transcripts need the speech recognition permission. Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are both on under Settings > Tesserae.
  • Handwriting lag: make sure you use Apple Pencil and the latest iPadOS. If it persists, email us with your device model and OS version.
  • New device: finish a backup on the old device, then restore on the new one.
