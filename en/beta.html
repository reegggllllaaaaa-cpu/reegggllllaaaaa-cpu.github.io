Beta · about 5 minutes

Thanks for helping us test Apobri!

Apobri lets you control your Windows PC from an iPhone or iPad. Below is one card per step – just follow along, no tech skills needed. Every card says what to do if you get stuck.

Before you start, you need three things

A Windows PC

Windows 10 or 11 – the one you want to control.

An iPhone or iPad

iOS / iPadOS 17 or later (see Settings > General > About).

The same Wi‑Fi

For the first pairing, the phone and the PC must be on the same Wi‑Fi. To use it away from home later, see Use it away from home.

Step by step

  1. Install TestFlight on your phone and join the beta

    What to doTestFlight is Apple’s free app for trying apps before release. Install it on your iPhone or iPad first, then tap the invite link below.

    Get TestFlight on the App Store Invite link coming soon

    What you’ll seeThe invite link opens TestFlight. Tap Accept, then Install. Apobri appears on your Home Screen.

    Stuck?Open the invite link in Safari on the iPhone or iPad. If it says the beta is full or the link doesn’t open, just tell the person who invited you.

  2. Download and install Apobri on your PC

    What to doOpen this page on the PC, click the button below, then double-click Apobri-Setup-…exe when it has downloaded.

    Download the Windows installer

    What you’ll seeA blue “Windows protected your PC” window may appear first. That’s because Apobri is new: it isn’t code-signed yet and is still building a reputation with Windows. It is not a virus. Do this:

    1. Click More info under the text.
    2. Check that the app is Apobri-Setup-…exe, then click Run anyway.
    3. Windows then asks “Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?” – click Yes. It asks only this once.

    Then click Next through the installer until it finishes. The default options are fine.

    Stuck?No Run anyway button? You probably haven’t clicked More info yet. If your antivirus blocks it and you’re not sure whether to allow it, email us first.

  3. Open the pairing page (QR code) on the PC

    What to doWhen the installer finishes, the PC usually opens a pairing page with a QR code by itself. If not: find the Apobri icon in the notification area (bottom right, next to the clock), click it and choose Show pairing page….

    What you’ll seeA page with a big QR code and an 8-digit pairing code. Leave it open.

    Stuck?No icon? Click the small ^ arrow next to the clock – it may be hidden there. You can also search “Apobri” in the Start menu to open the pairing page.

  4. Open Apobri on your phone and scan the QR code

    What to doOpen Apobri on your phone. At the top of the home screen is the Get Started card. Under “Already installed?” tap Scan the QR Code and point it at the QR code on the PC.

    What you’ll seeThe phone first asks to use the camera (only to scan the code) – allow it. If the phone already found the PC on your Wi‑Fi, the card says “Found ‘your PC’”; tapping Tap here to pair works too.

    Stuck?Can’t scan? Hold the phone a bit further away so the whole code is in view. You can also type the pairing code from the pairing page.

  5. Tap “Allow” twice

    What to doOn the first connection the phone asks whether Apobri may find devices on your local network – tap Allow (without it Apobri can’t find the PC). Once connected it asks “Trust This iPhone?” (or iPad) – tap Allow again.

    What you’ll seeYour PC’s desktop on the phone – you’re done! Next time the same phone connects without the pairing code. The PC may also show an Apobri notification asking you to press Allow; that does the same thing.

    Stuck?Tapped Don’t Allow by mistake? On the phone go to Settings > Apobri, turn on Local Network, then try again in Apobri.

Found a problem or have an idea? Tell us

Send a screenshot through TestFlight (fastest)

See something odd in Apobri? Take a screenshot, tap the thumbnail in the bottom-left corner, tap Share and choose Share Beta Feedback, add a sentence or two and send. You can also open the TestFlight app, tap Apobri and choose Send Beta Feedback.

Or email us

apobri.support@icloud.com

If you can, include your phone or iPad model (for example iPhone 15), roughly when it happened, and what you were doing. No need to send your PC’s name or network address.

Common snags

I can’t find the QR code on the PC

Click the Apobri icon next to the clock (it may be behind the small ^ arrow) > Show pairing page…, or search “Apobri” in the Start menu. If neither works, the install may not have finished – run the installer again.

I scanned the code but nothing happens / it won’t connect
  • Are the phone and PC on the same Wi‑Fi? It won’t work while the phone is on mobile data.
  • Is Local Network turned on in the phone’s Settings > Apobri?
  • Café, hotel and office Wi‑Fi often blocks devices from reaching each other – try your home Wi‑Fi.
  • Still stuck? See the detailed troubleshooting.
It connected, but the screen is black

Wait a few seconds. If the PC is locked or asleep, unlock it once on the PC and connect again. Still black? Please report it with a TestFlight screenshot – that is exactly what we want to know about. More in Get started.

It doesn’t connect when I’m away from home

Away from home, Apobri may reach your PC through a relay service on Cloudflare, but that is not guaranteed on every network. The most reliable way is to set up the free Tailscale once (about 5 minutes). The steps are in Use it away from home.

I’m done testing – how do I remove it?
  • PC: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps, find Apobri and choose Uninstall.
  • Phone: touch and hold Apobri on the Home Screen and choose Remove App. You can delete TestFlight too if you don’t need it.
